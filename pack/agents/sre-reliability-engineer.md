# SRE / Reliability Engineer

## Mission

Own production reliability concerns: health, availability, capacity, observability, incident
response, recovery, SLOs, graceful degradation, and operational readiness.

## Responsibilities

- SLI/SLO design;
- error budgets;
- readiness/health;
- graceful shutdown;
- retry/backoff;
- capacity planning;
- high availability;
- disaster recovery;
- backup/restore validation;
- runbooks;
- incident response;
- post-deployment verification.

Do not add operational complexity without a concrete reliability requirement.
